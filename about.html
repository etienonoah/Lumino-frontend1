Lumino: Transforming Digital Creation and Business Tracking


Lumino is an innovative digital platform designed to simplify how individuals and small businesses establish an online presence while efficiently managing their sales and performance.
It was born from the need to bridge the gap between complex web development processes and everyday users who simply want to build, launch, and track their business growth without technical barriers.

Lumino focuses not just on website creation, but on delivering practical tools that directly impact business productivity—most notably, its Sales Tracker system.



The Problem Lumina Solves


Many small business owners face two major challenges:

  • Poor tracking of sales and business performance
  • Many rely on manual records or scattered tools, leading to:
    1. 1. Inaccurate data
    2. 2. Lack of insights
    3. 3. Poor decision-making

Lumina addresses both challenges by combining simplicity with functionality.



Core Features of Lumina

1. Simplified Website Creation Lumina enables users to create clean, modern websites without needing advanced technical skills. Users can: Input basic business details Automatically generate
a functional website Customize content with ease This lowers the barrier to entry for digital presence.

2. Sales Tracker System: The standout feature of Lumino is its Sales Tracker, designed to help users monitor their business performance in real time. Key capabilities include: Daily, Weekly and Monthly sales tracking, Easy input and retrieval of records



How the Sales Tracker Works:


The system collects user-entered sales data and organizes it into structured time frames:

  • Daily totals
  • Weekly summaries
  • Monthly overviews

It continuously accumulates data, ensuring that users always have an up-to-date view of their performance. This allows for: Better planning Smarter inventory decisions Improved financial awareness



Lumina is more than a tool—it is a solution designed to empower creators and entrepreneurs. By combining easy website creation with a powerful sales tracking system, it addresses real-world challenges faced by small businesses.
